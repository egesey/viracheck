What it does

ViraCheck gathers the things every skipper repeats on every trip — from getting ready to writing the passage down.

Pre-departure checklists

Start with ready-made lists: before you cast off, safety equipment, engine and fuel. Trip readiness rises as you tick items off, so nothing is quietly skipped.

Wind, waves and sea state

Wind direction and speed for your position, gusts, Beaufort force, wave height and period, sea temperature and pressure — with an hourly wind strip and a daily summary.

Good to go, or not?

A judgement instead of a raw number: wind and gusts are read together and turned into a clear call — good to go, take care, or not today.

Nearby coves and routes

Coves and beaches around you, listed with their distance in nautical miles. Pick a few and the app orders them nearest-first and draws the total distance on the map.

Logbook

Record every passage: departure and arrival ports, times, distance, engine hours, fuel, crew and notes. Export the whole logbook as a PDF and share it whenever you need to.

Boat details and documents

Boat name, type, length, home port, call sign and MMSI in one place. Add expiry dates for insurance and registration, and get a reminder before they run out.

Ready when it matters

Finding the right words in a crisis is hard. The ViraCheck distress screen prepares a MAYDAY call already filled in with your boat's details.

  • A MAYDAY script with your boat name, MMSI and position in place — you only have to read it
  • One-tap calls to the coastguard (158) and emergency services (112)
  • VHF channel 16 reminder and when to use PAN-PAN instead
  • Man-overboard steps, in order

No account. No server.

You never sign up for ViraCheck, because there is nothing to sign up to.

  • Your checklists, logbook and boat details are stored only on your phone
  • We run no servers of our own — we do not collect, store or sell your data
  • No ad networks, no analytics, no user tracking
  • Your location is used only while a forecast or cove lookup is being made

Free and Pro

The whole app is usable for free. Pro lifts the limits.

Free

Checklists, current weather and sea state, the go/no-go call, the distress screen and the logbook — all usable, within sensible limits.

Pro

The 7-day wind forecast, the full 24-hour hourly strip, unlimited logbook entries, every ready-made checklist and all nearby coves. Monthly or yearly.
