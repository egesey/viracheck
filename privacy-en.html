ViraCheck is a mobile app that brings your pre-departure checks, logbook, and marine weather together in one place. This policy explains what data the app touches and what it does with it.

In short

  • ViraCheck has no accounts — you never sign up.
  • Everything you create stays on your device.
  • We run no servers of our own; we do not collect, store, or sell your data.
  • We use no ad networks, analytics, or user-tracking tools.

1. Data stored on your device

The following is kept only in your phone's local storage:

  • Checklists and the items you tick off
  • Logbook entries (departure and arrival port and time, distance, engine hours, fuel, crew, weather note, route notes)
  • Boat details
  • App settings

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us. If iCloud Backup is enabled on your device, this content may be included in Apple's backup, in which case Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

2. Location

We ask for location permission so the app can calculate wind, wave, and go/no-go information for where you actually are.

  • The app does not record, store, or keep a history of your location.
  • Your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo only while a forecast request is being made.
  • To display the name of your location on screen, the coordinates are passed to Apple's geocoding service through your device's operating system.
  • If you decline the permission, the rest of the app works normally — only the weather and sea state cannot be shown.
  • You can revoke the permission at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Weather and sea state

Forecast data comes from Open-Meteo. These requests carry only coordinates and the requested forecast fields; nothing identifying you is sent. Open-Meteo's terms: open-meteo.com/en/terms

4. Subscriptions and purchases

The ViraCheck Pro subscription is sold through the App Store and managed with RevenueCat.

  • Your payment details are handled by Apple and are never shared with us.
  • To track your subscription status, RevenueCat processes purchase information (product, date, renewal state) together with technical details such as device type and country, under a randomly generated anonymous user ID. Your name and email address are not sent.
  • RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple Media Services privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store

5. Notifications

The app uses only local notifications that run on your device (for example, a good-conditions reminder). Remote push infrastructure is disabled in the app; no data is sent to any server in order to deliver a notification.

6. Children's privacy

ViraCheck is not directed at users under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Deleting your data

You can reset your checklists and boat details from the relevant sections inside the app. To permanently erase all local data, simply remove the app from your device.

You can manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change. For significant changes, we will also notify you inside the app.

9. Contact

For privacy questions: egeseymen@icloud.com
